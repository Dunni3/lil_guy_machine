# How his world works, beyond the content

Hand-written sections of the world's docs for what lives in code rather than in the content files.
Each `## id: title` heading starts a section the oracle can open (agent/.../memory/Oracle.kt,
WorldDocs). Keep numbers out of here: prices, rates and rewards are in the generated sections, so
they can't drift.

## guide:making-art: how a piece of art gets made
When he makes a piece (a verb that produces art), he draws it himself, one choice at a time. First
he picks its subject: today's request is offered to him as one choice, beside a few subjects of his
own. Then he picks a box of crayons, then each stroke: the background, the subject's shape, then
details. Its title comes from the subject he picked. He can't choose what to draw before he starts
the verb; he chooses while drawing.

## guide:requests: meeting a request for a piece
A request names an adjective and a noun. A piece meets it only if it was made while the request was
open, its title or tags have the adjective, and a shape of the noun was drawn as its subject; a piece
titled for the request with no shape of the noun drawn doesn't count. Picking the request as the
subject is how to get both. Only the first matching piece sold earns the reward, and selling sells
every piece he holds at once.

## guide:hanging: hanging art on the wall
Any piece he made, a sketch or a painting, can be hung on the picture rail instead of sold. Every
hung piece raises the room's comfort by the same amount, whatever it shows, up to the gallery's
limit. Nothing takes a hung piece down, so it is never sold.

## guide:discovery: what he can't see yet
The shop only sells what he could use now; something that's no use without another thing appears
once he owns that thing. Books can teach him verbs and items he doesn't know yet; a book's section
names what it teaches, and finishing the book shows them to him.

## guide:no-verb: what he can't do
He can only do what a verb does. If no verb does something (take art down, cook, go outside), he
can't do it.
