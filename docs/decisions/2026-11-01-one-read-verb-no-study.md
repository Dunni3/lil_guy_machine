---
title: "One read verb: a new chapter raises the book's skill; there is no study"
date: 2026-11-01
status: active
---

**Context.** The knowledge-and-skill decision split books into `read` (for the story: mood and
stimulation) and `study` (for the skill, at the desk). Both moved the same chapter counter, so they
were one activity with two payoff mixes. Each wore on its own, and alternating between them was a
way round habituation that his self-model found and wrote down ("alternating between different
study topics prevents diminished returns"). In a 2-day Jev run, books were the pick in 16 of 37
decisions.

**Decision.** `study` is gone; `read` does both.

- The `progress` op has no `study` flag. Reading a new chapter raises the book's skills; rereading a
  finished book gives no progress and no skill. A book's skills are what read's gains grow with.
- `read`: 60 min at the bed or chair, energy -2, mood +2, stimulation +6, `enjoy`, `wear: verb`.
- The `study` verb kind stays (play_guitar uses it). The desk-study pose and its open book are gone
  from the app; reading in his hands is the one book animation.

**Consequences.** One habit for all books. A book's first-time novelty is still per book, so a new
book still pays it; that's the next lever if he still reads too much. Supersedes the read/study
split in [2026-10-31-knowledge-and-skill-books-teach-skills-pay.md](2026-10-31-knowledge-and-skill-books-teach-skills-pay.md).
