The Field Lab

A small house that is half home and half laboratory, a garden, a patch of forest to take samples from, and a rocket going up in the yard, one stage at a time.

The Field Lab

Click the ground to walk him there, or a numbered stake in the forest to take that sample. Drag to look around, scroll to zoom. The house opens when he is inside or headed in.

The idea

The cute isometric rooms from the first prototype, with the same butter-yellow guy and his sprout, moved somewhere a little further on in time. The future is in small things he has made: a lantern that follows him, a lifter that hoists rocket stages, a flower that tracks the sun, an analyser with an amber screen. The rest is wood, plaster, rugs and plants.

Three loops are sketched: collect a sample and shelve it, add the next piece of the lab, add the next stage of the rocket.

What is faked

  • Nothing here is wired to the sim. No needs, money or model; he does what you click.
  • Building is a button: no parts, cost or time. Lab pieces come out of three crates, rocket stages wait in the yard.
  • Samples are six fixed spots. Jars are for show; the microscope and analyser do nothing yet.
  • The cutaway is the two near walls sinking and the roof fading. Walking is A* on a half-tile grid.
  • Evening is seven small point lights and glowing panes, not real light through windows. The rocket does not launch.
