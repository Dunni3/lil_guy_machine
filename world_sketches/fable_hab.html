The unit

Him. A person, cut from flat planes in one colour like a vinyl figure: jacket shoulders, long coat tail, heavy boots, a mohawk of fins. He wears shades, and his mood is the two lights on them: bars, chevrons, flat lines, hollow squares.

The place. Earth, 2226. Dark flat-shaded rooms on a gantry two hundred floors up, with the city and its traffic below in the haze. Each room has one accent colour, and that colour is the only light in it that glows. The walls carry what he'd put on them: stencils, tape, stickers, his own pixel pieces on light panels.

One room at a time, again. The module he's in is solid. Every other one is a wireframe of itself. Changing rooms un-prints one from the top down and prints the next from the floor up, and he goes through the same scanner. A room he hasn't paid for is the same wireframe with a price on it.

What's faked

  • Clock, log, needs and credits are canned. Nothing talks to the sim.
  • He walks straight through furniture, and re-prints between modules instead of using a door.
  • Bloom is a post pass. Not measured headless or on the TV, where it would be the first thing to cost.

How it could plug in

  • Same house data. A module is a list of boxes on a 6×6 floor plus wall decals, the shape house.js already has. The wireframe is built from those same boxes, so a blueprint costs nothing to author.
  • Rise becomes print height. Round two's one number per room (how far it stands up) is one clip plane per module here. Focus, buying and his arrival all drive it.
  • Snap-on rooms are slots on the gantry. A new module is the next slot. The nav strip is the closed choice Jev makes.
  • The HUD is the sim's own state: needs as segment bars, the day memory as a log, money as credits. It's HTML over the canvas, so the TV's WebView gets it for free.
  • His art is the decor. The light panels in the studio and bunk are slots for what ArtStudio saves.
  • Accent per module is one colour in content YAML, and it drives the glow, the HUD and the wireframe together.

What I'd try next

  • A second body plan or two for him at this fidelity, side by side, before settling the silhouette.
  • Him tagging a wall himself: the writer model picks the words and they stay.
  • The roof as a real exterior you can walk the gantry from, module to module.
